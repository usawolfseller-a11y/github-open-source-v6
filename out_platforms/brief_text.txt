🛰️ <b>AI Brief — SpaceX xAI Releases Grok Bot AI Teammates with Persistent Cloud Computers</b>
──────────────
🧭 <b>What happened</b>
▸ Launched Grok Bot for macOS, Windows, Linux, iOS, and Android.
▸ Provided each Bot with a persistent cloud computer featuring a browser, filesystem, and terminal.
▸ Enabled Bots to learn workflows from demonstration, run routines on a schedule, and coordinate in shared group chats.

🎯 <b>Why developers should care</b>
Grok Bot introduces persistent cloud-based AI agents capable of browser interaction, file management, and terminal execution, offering developers a flexible multi-agent environment with cross-app workflows, custom skills, and scheduled routines.

🔍 <b>Confirmed vs. video-only</b>
✅ Grok Bot runs on macOS, Windows, Linux, iOS, and Android.
✅ Each Bot operates on a persistent cloud computer with a browser, filesystem, and terminal.
✅ Bots can learn workflows from demonstration and run on schedules.
◻️ Video-only: Specific demonstrations involving scout bots finding specific AI news articles and shopper bots interacting with Best Buy carts.
